Weeks 2–6 · The Gold Standard Signal

Close the Conversion Loop — Offline Tracking & Call Attribution

Phases 1 and 2 restore functional tracking. Phase 3 is what moves the account from recovering to genuinely optimised. The difference between a A$10.86 blended CPA and a A$6.28 CPA runs through this phase.

Monday.com → Zapier → Google Ads Offline Conversion
The account currently tells Google Ads "this person submitted a form." With offline conversion import, we tell it "this person submitted a form, booked a job, and paid A$349." Smart Bidding then optimises toward actual revenue — not lead volume.

Technical flow: User clicks ad → GCLID generated → GTM captures GCLID in cookie → user submits form → GCLID + UTM passed as hidden fields into Monday.com → Peter marks job "Booked/Won" → Zapier fires → Google Ads Offline Conversion API receives GCLID + actual job value.

Conversion window: 7 days. Jay confirmed speed to lead is under 15 minutes and bookings confirm same day. 7 days covers edge cases without polluting signal with late-attributed conversions.

ROAS bidding trigger: Once 90 days of offline data with 30+ confirmed jobs per campaign — switch Brisbane and Gold Coast to Target ROAS.
Aircall Attribution — Call Source Tracking
~67% of leads come via phone. Jay confirmed phone leads close faster and at a higher rate than form fills. At peak times, inbound calls take priority over form submissions. Team calls back form fills 3× back-to-back then sends an SMS — more friction and more drop-off.

Action from Jay: Confirm whether Aircall's native Google Ads integration is active. If yes, call data flows back automatically. If not, implement manual reconciliation: Aircall number → campaign mapping in a tracking sheet, reconciled weekly with Google Ads click data.

Phone vs form values: Phone call conversions warrant a higher conversion value than form fills for the same service — Jay confirmed the differential close rate (Q11). Assign A$65 to calls vs A$52 to form fills for Roadworthy as a starting point.

Primary metrics are CPA and actual booked jobs. Not CTR. Not impressions. Not optimisation score.

Reported monthly: CVR by service type · Revenue per lead (once offline flows) · Campaign ROAS (once 90 days of offline data available).

Format: WhatsApp group update weekly (5 bullets max) · Looker Studio dashboard shared with Jay, Peter, Joel.
The GA4 vs Google Ads Gap — Ongoing Flag
GA4 and Google Ads use different attribution models and different conversion windows. The gap will narrow as clean data accumulates. If it remains large after 30 days of clean tracking, it points to a remaining duplicate conversion action or tag firing issue — investigate the container before escalating.
SEO / Paid Keyword Alignment — Ongoing with Jai
SR Digital is building dedicated PPC landing pages — there is no URL dependency on Jai's organic pages. The alignment needed is at the keyword level only: ensure paid campaigns are not buying traffic for keywords Jai's organic work already converts at a lower cost, and ensure paid copy does not conflict with organic brand positioning.

Protocol: Bi-weekly alignment review. Share paid keyword performance data with Jai — flag any terms where organic position has improved to top 3 and paid spend on that term may be redundant.
